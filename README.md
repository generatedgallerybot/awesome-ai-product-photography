## Background Removal

- [remove.bg](https://www.remove.bg/) - One-click AI background removal with clean edges. Industry standard for quick cutouts.
- [Photoroom](https://www.photoroom.com/) - AI-powered background removal and replacement designed for ecommerce. Batch processing support.
- [Clipping Magic](https://clippingmagic.com/) - Precise AI background removal with fine-tuning controls for hair, edges, and transparency.
- [Pixelcut](https://www.pixelcut.ai/) - AI photo editor with instant background removal, magic eraser, and image upscaling for product photos.

## Full Product Shoots

- [Flair.ai](https://flair.ai/) - AI design tool for branded product photography. Drag and drop products into AI-generated scenes.
- [Claid.ai](https://claid.ai/) - AI-powered product image generation and enhancement API. Bulk processing for ecommerce catalogs.
- [AdCreator.ai](https://adcreator.ai/) - Generate studio-quality product photos and ad creatives from a single product image. Supports lifestyle scenes, flat lays, and branded templates.
- [Pebblely](https://pebblely.com/) - AI product photo generator — upload a product image and generate beautiful lifestyle backgrounds instantly.
- [CreatorKit](https://creatorkit.com/) - AI product photo and video generator built for Shopify. Generate backgrounds and lifestyle images at scale.
- [Booth.ai](https://www.booth.ai/) - Professional product photography using AI. Upload your product, get studio-quality images in multiple styles.

## Ad Creative

- [AdCreative.ai](https://www.adcreative.ai/) - AI-powered ad creative generator for social media and display ads. A/B testing with performance scoring.
- [Pencil](https://www.trypencil.com/) - AI ad creative platform that generates and tests video and static ads using brand assets.
- [Predis.ai](https://predis.ai/) - AI content generator for social media posts, carousels, and video ads from text prompts.
- [Canva Magic Studio](https://www.canva.com/magic/) - AI-powered design suite with Magic Design, background remover, and text-to-image for ad creatives.

## Style Transfer & Scene Generation

- [Stylized](https://www.stylized.ai/) - AI product photography tool that transforms plain product photos into styled scenes with custom backgrounds.
- [Mokker AI](https://mokker.ai/) - AI background generator for product photos. Upload a product and generate dozens of professional backgrounds.
- [ZMO.ai](https://www.zmo.ai/) - AI image generator with product placement, model generation, and background creation for ecommerce.

## Video Creation

- [Synthesia](https://www.synthesia.io/) - AI video generator with realistic avatars. Create product demo videos from text scripts.
- [HeyGen](https://www.heygen.com/) - AI video generation platform for product ads and explainers with customizable AI avatars.
- [Oxolo](https://www.oxolo.com/) - AI video generator that creates product marketing videos from a URL. Auto-generates script, voiceover, and visuals.

## Image Enhancement

- [Let's Enhance](https://letsenhance.io/) - AI image upscaling and enhancement. Increase resolution up to 16x while maintaining quality.
- [Topaz Photo AI](https://www.topazlabs.com/topaz-photo-ai) - Desktop AI tool for noise reduction, sharpening, and upscaling product images.
